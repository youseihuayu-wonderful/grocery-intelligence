# Market Research — Grocery AI / Applied ML Engineer

Research conducted: March 25, 2026

---

## Industry Overview

### Online Grocery Market Size

| Year | Market Size | Growth |
|------|-----------|--------|
| 2025 | $655.51B | — |
| 2026 | $794.86B | +21.3% YoY |
| 2030 | ~$1.5T (projected) | CAGR ~20% |
| 2035 | $2,237.1B (projected) | continued growth |

### AI in Food Retail & E-commerce

- Market growing at **30.8% CAGR** through 2030, reaching **$13.4 billion**
- Retail AI overall: $11.6B (2024) → $40.7B (2030), CAGR 23%
- **92% of retailers** using AI for personalized shopping
- **45.8% of consumers** willing to use AI chatbots for grocery shopping
- **32.6% of shoppers** willing to let AI auto-reorder staple items

## Competitive Landscape

### Major Players Investing in Grocery AI

| Company | AI Initiative |
|---------|--------------|
| **Instacart** | Launched ChatGPT-powered grocery shopping app. AI-driven product recommendations. Provides AI tech to retail partners. |
| **DoorDash** | Released competing ChatGPT grocery app one week after Instacart. Expanding grocery delivery with AI. |
| **Kroger** | Growing dependent on third-party AI/e-commerce vendors (Instacart, DoorDash, Uber) |
| **Amazon Fresh** | Deep ML-powered recommendation and search systems |
| **Walmart** | Investing in AI for inventory, search, and delivery optimization |

### Key Trend: AI Arms Race

Instacart and DoorDash are both racing to deploy AI-powered shopping assistants, proving this is a high-demand area with real business value.

## Job Market for This Skillset

### Demand Growth

- AI/ML Engineer roles: **+41.8% year-over-year**
- Quarterly growth: **+13.1%**
- LLM fine-tuning: **most in-demand AI skill**
- Applied ML / AI Integration roles are in high demand

### Salary Ranges (US Market, 2025-2026)

| Role | Average | Senior Level |
|------|---------|-------------|
| AI Engineer | $206,000 | $250K-$312K |
| LLM Engineer | $156,329 | $200K-$312K |
| ML Engineer (E-commerce) | $150K-$180K | $200K-$280K |
| Applied Scientist | $160K-$200K | $220K-$350K |

### Why This Project Matters for Job Seekers

1. **Proves base model adaptation skills** — the #1 thing employers want
2. **Combines multiple ML techniques** — shows system design ability
3. **Uses real data** — demonstrates practical engineering, not just theory
4. **Directly relevant** to Instacart, DoorDash, Amazon Fresh, Kroger, and other grocery tech companies
